Emergency Action Checklist

  • ✓ Disconnect: Turn off internet / Wi-Fi if malware is suspected.
  • ✓ Block Accounts: Contact your bank immediately to freeze affected cards or UPI handles.
  • ✓ Preserve Evidence: Take screenshots of transactions, SMS, WhatsApp chats, and caller IDs. Do not delete them.
  • ✓ Secure Access: Change passwords from a different, secure device. Enable MFA.

AUREXA Incident Advisory

While AUREXA provides threat intelligence tools for enterprises, we are not a government entity. If you are an individual victim of fraud, you must use official government channels.

Common Scams to Watch Out For:
Fake Customer Care: Scammers posting fake toll-free numbers on Google search results.
APK Fraud (Parcel/Electricity Scams): SMS claiming delivery delay or electricity cut, asking you to download an APK file which steals SMS OTPs.
Part-time Job Scams: Telegram/WhatsApp messages offering money for liking YouTube videos, eventually asking for "investments".
