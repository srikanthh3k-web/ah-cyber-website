Cyber Fraud Help & Reporting

Immediate Actions for Victims of Fraud

1. Block Financial Assets

Immediately contact your bank or UPI provider to freeze your accounts and block compromised cards.

2. Preserve Evidence

Do not delete malicious emails, SMS, or WhatsApp messages. Take screenshots of transactions and fake profiles.

3. Secure Accounts

Change passwords immediately from a secure device and enable Multi-Factor Authentication (MFA).

4. Official Reporting (India)

Report the incident to the official National Cyber Crime Reporting Portal.
