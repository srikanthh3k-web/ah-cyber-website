CYBER FRAUD ASSISTANCE

Think You've Been Scammed?

If you have lost money, shared sensitive information, clicked a suspicious link or installed an unknown APK, take action quickly.

✓ Save screenshots, transaction details and messages.
✓ Contact your bank or payment provider immediately.
✓ Do not share OTPs, passwords or recovery codes.
✓ Report cybercrime through the official Indian portal.
🚨 Report Cyber Crime

Emergency Safety Checklist

💳 Financial Fraud Contact your bank immediately and preserve transaction evidence.
📱 Malicious APK Disconnect if necessary and avoid opening the application again.
🔑 Account Compromise Change credentials from a trusted device and enable MFA.
🌐 Phishing Do not enter credentials into suspicious websites.
CYBER SECURITY UPDATES

Security Awareness

Important security topics users should watch for.

PHISHING

Fake Login Pages

Attackers can imitate banking, social media and email login pages to steal credentials.

MOBILE SECURITY

Unknown APK Files

Avoid installing APK files received from unknown sources, especially files promising rewards or refunds.

FINANCIAL SAFETY

OTP & UPI Scams

Never disclose OTPs, PINs or authorization codes to callers claiming to be bank representatives.

SOCIAL ENGINEERING

Urgency Is a Red Flag

Messages demanding immediate payment or threatening account closure deserve extra scrutiny.

PASSWORD SECURITY

Use Strong Authentication

Use unique passwords and multi-factor authentication for important accounts.

PRIVACY

Protect Personal Data

Avoid sharing identity documents, passwords and sensitive information with unverified contacts.
