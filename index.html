Emergency Action Steps

  • Disconnect: Turn off internet/Wi-Fi immediately if remote access malware is suspected.
  • Block Financial Assets: Contact your bank immediately to freeze credit cards, debit cards, and UPI handles.
  • Preserve Evidence: Take screenshots of transactions, SMS, WhatsApp chats, URLs, and caller IDs. Do not delete them.
  • Secure Accounts: Change passwords from a different, known-safe device. Enable Multi-Factor Authentication (MFA).
