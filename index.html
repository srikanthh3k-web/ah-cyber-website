CYBER FRAUD ASSISTANCE

Cyber Fraud Help & Reporting



If you are facing online fraud, preserve evidence and report it quickly.











🚨 Suspected Cyber Fraud?




Use Aurexa as an awareness and guidance layer. Do not share OTPs,
passwords, PINs or banking credentials with anyone.








1. Preserve Evidence

Screenshots, transaction IDs, phone numbers, emails and URLs.





2. Contact Your Bank

Immediately report unauthorized financial transactions.





3. Report Cyber Crime

Use the official Indian cybercrime reporting channels.

🛡 Aurexa Safety Checklist




Follow these basic practices to reduce your cyber-risk.







✓ Enable multi-factor authentication


✓ Use unique strong passwords


✓ Verify links before opening


✓ Never share OTP/PIN/CVV


✓ Keep operating systems updated


✓ Monitor financial transactions
