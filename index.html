CYBER FRAUD ASSISTANCE



Cyber Fraud Help & Reporting




If you face online fraud, preserve evidence and report it quickly.














🚨 Suspected Cyber Fraud?




Do not share OTPs, passwords, PINs, CVV or banking
credentials with anyone.





1. Preserve Evidence

Screenshots, transaction IDs, phone numbers,
emails and URLs.





2. Contact Your Bank

Immediately report unauthorized transactions.





3. Report Cyber Crime

Use the official Indian cybercrime reporting channel.

🛡 Cyber Safety Checklist




Basic practices to reduce your online security risk.




✓ Enable multi-factor authentication


✓ Use unique strong passwords


✓ Verify links before opening


✓ Never share OTP/PIN/CVV


✓ Keep software updated


✓ Monitor financial transactions
