Cyber Fraud Help

Immediate defensive steps for common online fraud situations.

🎣 Phishing

Do not enter credentials into suspicious links. Verify the website independently before signing in.

💳 Payment Fraud

Contact your bank/payment provider immediately and preserve transaction information and screenshots.

📱 Account Compromise

Change passwords from a trusted device, revoke unknown sessions and enable multi-factor authentication.

📦 Suspicious APK

Do not install APKs from untrusted sources. Review permissions and scan the file with trusted security software.

🧾 Preserve Evidence

Keep URLs, transaction IDs, emails, messages, screenshots and relevant timestamps.

🇮🇳 India Cyber Fraud

For suspected cybercrime in India, use official government reporting channels or contact the appropriate authorities.
